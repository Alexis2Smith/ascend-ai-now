Acknowledged Does Not Equal Controlled

October 2026

Governance requires more than risk acknowledgment. Recognizing a risk is an event; control requires capabilities that can detect, contain, monitor, and stop an unsafe process when needed.

Acknowledgment is an event. Control is an outcome.
  • Detection and escalation are not equivalent to stopping an unsafe process.
  • Evaluate containment and termination capabilities before deployment.
  • Set monitoring severity to match the impact and operating context.
  • Map environment coverage, including relevant integrations and operating modes.
  • Require evidence that safeguards work in practice, including when systems fail or behave unexpectedly.
