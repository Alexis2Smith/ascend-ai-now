Ascend AI NOW™ Framework for Responsible AI

Responsibility, designed at every stage

A five-stage framework for closing the AI Power Gap™ with governance, architecture, deployment, and accountability moving together.

Diagnose→Govern→Architect→Deploy→Scale

Framework visuals: each stage uses its approved original artwork, presented without cropping or redesign.

Stage 01

Diagnose

See the AI Power Gap™ before you scale it.

Establish a clear view of current AI use, readiness, constraints, and the conditions that must be understood before an organization expands adoption.

Stage 02

Govern

Define authority. Build accountability. Include affected voices.

The Ascend AI Governance Compass centers decision rights, oversight, accountability, and participation by people affected by AI-enabled decisions.

Stage 03

Architect

Design responsibility into every layer.

Shape data, platforms, integrations, controls, and human workflows so responsibility is considered in the system design rather than added as an afterthought.

Stage 04

Deploy

Create agency, not just access.

Prepare people to use AI with informed choice, relevant skills, appropriate human oversight, and clear routes to question or escalate a result.

Stage 05

Scale

Do not scale AI faster than accountability.

Expand only as oversight, monitoring, response capabilities, and evidence keep pace with the system's reach and impact.
