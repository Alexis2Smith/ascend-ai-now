About Ascend AI NOW™

Human judgment at the center of every AI decision

Ascend AI NOW is an AI governance, readiness, and strategic implementation firm based in the Research Triangle, North Carolina, serving clients worldwide. We pair specialist AI with accountable human leadership so institutions can adopt AI with confidence, and without leaving people behind.

Where the concept came from

Every new technology reaches the people with the most first. We exist so AI is different.

The AI Power Gap™ is the distance between the institutions that can govern AI well and the people those institutions serve. Our founder saw that gap up close for three decades, long before AI arrived.

2007

Rural school district technology leadership

Led technology for a rural Alabama school district, where access was never guaranteed and every decision touched students directly.

2012

Municipal government and public infrastructure

Delivered city systems and fiber-to-home projects, learning how technology choices shape whole communities.

2016

Utility cooperatives across eight states

Guided technology programs for more than 30 rural electric cooperatives.

2018

District Chief Technology Officer

Led district technology operations supporting more than 30,000 students and 45,000 devices, and secured more than $2M in federal E-Rate funding.

2020

Enterprise architecture in healthcare

Managed IT architecture for a major health insurer: 37 architects, 45+ workstreams, and an enterprise Microsoft Copilot rollout for 5,000+ users.

2025

Ascend AI NOW is founded

Doctoral research in Digital Stewardship, with a concentration in AI Ethics and Governance, becomes a practice: the AI Power Gap™ Framework and the Ascend AI NOW™ Framework for Responsible AI.

2026

The AI Executive Command Center™

We build our own operating model the way we advise clients to: specialist AI in defined seats, with a human governance layer over every output.

How we work

One team. Every angle. AI-powered. Human-accountable.

Our AI Executive Command Center puts each AI tool in a defined role, then routes every result through accountable people. It is how we deliver faster without lowering the bar.

01

Specialist AI in defined seats

Research, strategy, design, equity review, immersive development, and video each have a dedicated AI specialist with a clear scope.

02

A human governance layer

Decision rights, risk management, evidence chains, accountability, and ethics review sit with people, not models.

03

Accountable outcomes

Every deliverable is reviewed and signed off by a named human lead. Client data is never used to train AI models.

Meet the founder

Dr. Alexis S. Smith

Technology executive · AI governance strategist · Educator

Dr. Alexis S. Smith brings 30+ years in technology, with leadership roles since 2007 as a two-time district Chief Technology Officer, an enterprise IT architecture leader, and now Founder and CEO of Ascend AI NOW, LLC. She is an AI governance strategist and educator. Her leadership spans K–12 education, public safety, municipal government, and healthcare, bridging enterprise technology and responsible AI innovation.

A former Chief Technology Officer for Montgomery Public Schools and Bullock County Schools in Alabama, she led district technology operations supporting over 30,000 students and 45,000 devices. She subsequently led enterprise solution architecture at Blue Cross Blue Shield of North Carolina.

Dr. Smith serves as a Global Ethical AI Delegate for the USA Chapter of Women in Ethical AI, CoSN Thought Partner and K–12 CTO Academy Mentor, Fractional Chief AI Officer for MedCore, and Adjunct Professor of Artificial Intelligence at Carolina Christian College. She is a member of Black Women in Artificial Intelligence (BWIAI) and builds AI content-safety solutions using NVIDIA APIs.

She created the Ascend AI NOW™ Framework for Responsible AI, AI Power Gap™ Readiness Index, and AI Governance Velocity Gap™ assessment. She publishes Closing the AI Power Gap™ and authored The AI Power Gap™: Governance Capacity in Enterprise Adoption (DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.22017354).

Her work advances equitable, accountable AI adoption, particularly in under-resourced educational communities.

Dr. Smith holds a Doctorate in Digital Stewardship (AI Ethics and Governance), is a Certified Executive Coach and TAIMScore™ Certified Assessor, and is a 2026 Global Ethical AI Congress Leadership Award honoree and Black Leaders Worldwide Woman to Watch.
